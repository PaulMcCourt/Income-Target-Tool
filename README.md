

# 💰 How Much to Live Where — Income Target Tool

Interactive Tableau dashboard designed to estimate required income based on state and family structure.

## 📌 Project Overview

This tool answers a simple but important question:

> *"How much do I need to earn to live where I want, given my family needs to maintain a modest lifestyle?"*

Users can explore required income across the United States by filtering on:

- 📍 **Geographic location** — State and County
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Household structure** — Number of adults and children
- 📅 **Timeframe** — Annual or monthly expense view

The dashboard empowers users to compare required income, expense distributions, and cost of living across different states and counties side by side.

## 📁 Data Source

Based on the **Economic Policy Institute (EPI) Family Budget Calculator**, which provides cost of living estimates across U.S. metro and non-metro areas for families of different sizes.

🔗 [EPI Family Budget Dataset](https://www.epi.org/resources/budget/)
